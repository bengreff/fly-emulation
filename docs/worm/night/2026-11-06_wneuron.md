## Unit-check bars (written before running)

**Row 4, written 00:05, before any run.** `scripts/worm/channels_unit_check.py`. Protocol is Nicoletti 2024's AIY
current clamp as published (`AIY_simulation_iclamp.py`): one compartment of 65.89 um2, steps of -15 to +35 pA
(11 steps), 1000 to 6000 ms, dt 0.4 ms, start at -60 mV, ek -80, eca 60. Steady state = mean V over 5990 to
6000 ms; peak = min V over 1000 to 1300 ms for the 3 hyperpolarising steps, max V for the rest. The reference is
Nicoletti's published mods in their own process.
- **A (the port, pass/fail):** Nicoletti's mods, but with wormemu's kqt1 and slo1iso (nmodl/channels) in place of
  theirs. Kinetics come from the channel_library Specs, densities from dens_kqt1_AIY and dens_slo1iso_AIY,
  cai_to_uM = 1 and cai 0.05 uM (the published cai0 of 5e-5 mM). **Pass if steady-state and peak V are within
  0.1 mV of the reference at every step.**
- **A0 (does it matter):** A without the two added channels. Reported, not pass/fail. If A0 is also within 1 mV,
  the added channels do not matter for AIY and the check is weak, and I say so.
- **W (the brain's library):** the same cell built from what the brain loads. That is BAAIWorm's egl19,
  slo1_egl19, nca and shl1 mods at their own defaults, pas for leak, and wormemu's kqt1 and slo1iso, with every
  density at Nicoletti's AIY value. **Pass if within 2 mV at every step.** A fail here would be a difference
  between BAAIWorm's 2019 channel models and Nicoletti's 2024 ones, not a fault in the port.
- RIM: Nicoletti's RIM fit has none of the five channels (their published density is 0), so a RIM check would test
  nothing added. AFD has no Nicoletti fit. Neither is run.
