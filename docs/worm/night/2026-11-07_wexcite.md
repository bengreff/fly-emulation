## Item 0: single-cell sanity (measured, 7 Nov 01:35-01:43)

Script `scripts/worm/single_cell_sanity.py`; per-cell table `data/worm/excite/single_cell_sanity.csv` (302 rows, four
configurations). One Brain with all 302 neurons and the wiring removed, so every cell is alone. Inputs and
cross-cell mechanisms are off (senses, peptides, amines, receptors, glia, organs). The membrane stays at its
defaults: channels, Ca pool, pumps, temperature, homeostasis. Steps: 1.5 s rest, then 1 s at -20 to +40 pA by 10.
It takes 11 s of wall per step for all 302 cells.

Bars (written in the script before the first run):
- **Upper (scored):** V stays at or below the highest reversal present at that moment (E_Na 50 mV, the soma's own E_Ca, e_pas).
- **Lower:** the brief's "V above the lowest reversal" is not a law under injected current. The recorded cells break it (measured, Naudin 2022 training sweeps at -15 pA: AIY -126.3 mV, RIM -119.2 mV, AFD -94.6 mV, against E_K -77 mV). So it is reported only. Scored instead: V at or above -150 mV at -20 pA (derived from AIY).

Results:
- **Finite everywhere (pass).**
- **Upper bar: 74 of 302 cells fail.** By the first step that breaks them: 4 at +10 pA (AIA, RIB), 27 at +20 (including AIY, ADA, AIB, RIS, RMD, RIP), 27 at +30, 16 at +40. The worst is RIBL at +333.9 mV (+40 pA). E_Ca at those moments is about +134 mV.
- **-150 mV floor at -20 pA: 47 fail.** AIYL reaches -369 mV.
- Rest at 0 pA: median -54.6 mV, range -79.3 to -7.0 mV. Five cells rest above -30 mV: RIPR -7.0, RIPL -15.1, AVBL/R -26, RIBL -29.8.
- Input resistance at -10 pA (derived): median 3.6 GOhm, 10-90% 1.9-6.7 GOhm. AIYL is 13.7 GOhm against 4.8 GOhm for the recorded AIY (derived from its -15 pA training sweep).

Causes, one configuration change at a time (each a full 302-cell run):

|---|---|---|---|
| default (ledger values, expression_map on) | 74 | 47 | 5 |
| expression_map off (BAAIWorm's per-cell channel sets) | 13 | 92 | 0 |
| also literature_overrides off (BAAIWorm as published; E_Ca then sits near +42 mV, so its bar is lower) | 66 | 92 | 5 |

At dt 0.25 ms the 9 violators match dt 0.5 within 0.2 mV: max V at +40 pA moves by at most 0.2 mV, min V at -20 pA by at most 0.2 mV.

- **Not numerics, not units.**
  - The time step changes nothing (last table row).
  - E_Ca of +134 mV at rest is the correct Nernst value for 2 mM out and 0.05 uM in.
  - The Nicoletti channels' cai unit is the uM edit, as documented.
  - No NaN anywhere.
  - So I fixed nothing.
- **61 of the 74 upper violators: the expression map (parameter-level, Open).**
  - `Brain._density` applies CeNGEN presence at threshold 2 on top of BAAIWorm's per-cell fits, without refitting. It zeroes fitted channels the class does not detect, and gives a detected channel BAAIWorm left at 0 the median density.
  - In BAAIWorm's raw fits, 73 of the 74 violators carry all 7 voltage-gated K types (shk1, egl36, kvs1, shl1, egl2, kqt3, irk). After the mask they carry 0-5 (median 3), while gaining Ca channels.
  - Example, AIYL:
    - Loses egl2, shl1, kqt3, kvs1, irk, egl36, shk1 and cca1 (CeNGEN 0 TPM).
    - Gains unc2 at 0.001 S/cm2 (0.23 nS; CeNGEN 93.9 TPM, BAAIWorm 0).
    - Keeps only Ca-activated K, KQT-1 and a 0.012 nS leak. Nicoletti's AIY leak is 0.14 nS.
  - So the mask removes the outward conductance each fit balanced against. FIT2's fitted map is the replacement.
- **The other 13 fail in BAAIWorm's own per-cell fits (parameter-level, Open).**
  - The cells: RIBL/R, LUAL/R, RMDL/R, RMDVL/R, RMHL/R, SAADL, SAAVL/R.
  - With the expression map off they still pass E_Ca at +30 or +40 pA (max 141-213 mV). Their input resistance is 5.6-7.8 GOhm: too little outward conductance for 30-40 pA.
  - For scale, the recorded RIM reaches +75 mV at +35 pA (measured).

## Items 1-2: FIT2 pre-registration and build (01:46-01:58)

- `docs/worm/FIT2.md` is the registration: one map from CeNGEN expression to somatic channel density and leak, shared
  by every class. 19 parameters (17 family scales, one expression slope b, one leak reversal), each bounded by the
  published per-cell fits (BAAIWorm's 302 and Nicoletti 2024's). No per-cell knobs.
- Training: AIY, AFD, AWA, six sweeps each (-15 to +35 pA by 10). Held out and sealed: RIM's six FIT2 sweeps, drawn by
  seed 20261107 at 01:51:50. The fit code never opens `Fig1A_RIM_CC.txt`; only `fit2_eval.py` does, once.
- Falsifier: 4 shuffled-CeNGEN fits (each class gets another class's whole profile) with the identical budget. The true
  map must beat all four on RIM. By chance an uninformative map passes 1 time in 5 (derived), so this is a weak test.
- Exposure before the draw is listed in FIT2.md section 6 (min/max of RIM and AFD sweeps 0 and 10, the first 3 AWA
  samples, AIY's -126.3 mV).
- Checks (measured): applying parameters to a built brain matches a fresh build at the same values (0.0 mV); one
  evaluation (3 cells x 6 sweeps x 9 s) takes 11.4 s on the Mac and 32.8 s on one backhouse core.
- At the template the model is far too excitable: AWA reaches +242 mV at +35 pA, and its input resistance is about
  12 GOhm against about 1 GOhm for the recorded AWA (derived from its -15 pA sweep, -14.8 mV).
