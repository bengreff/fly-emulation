## Step 1: Randi et al. 2023 (Nature 623:406, PMC10632145, read 22:10)

- Strains: wild-type atlas AML462 (pan-neuronal GUR-3/PRDX-2 + nuclear GCaMP6s + NeuroPAL); unc-31 AML508
  (unc-31(wtf502), same imaging); control without GUR-3 AML320 (the null for q values).
- n: 113 wild-type animals, 18 unc-31 animals (on backhouse: 113 and 18 recordings in exported_data*). 23,433 pairs
  measured, 1,310 with q < 0.05 (6%). funatlas.h5 (wormneuroatlas): dFF[i, j] = response of i to stimulation of j;
  wt has 173 stimulated neurons with trials and 208 responders; unc-31 109 stimulated.
- Stimulation: two-photon, 850 nm, 500 kHz, a 500 ms train (wt) or 300 ms (unc-31), every 30 s.
- Imaging: 2 volumes/s. Immobilised: 10% agarose, 100 nm beads, levamisole (2 ul of 500 uM stock).
- Statistic: mean dF/F0 over a 30 s window after the stimulation, F0 = mean F over the 30 s before; averaged over
  trials and animals. q from two-sided KS tests (amplitude and its second derivative) against the AML320 null.
  Events kept only if the target showed a detected autoresponse (classifier on dF/F and its second derivative).
  Kernels: sums of convolved decaying exponentials (funatlas kernels g, factor, power_t, branch).

## How the morning report reads the fit

- One command, no sealed data:
  `ssh backhouse 'wsl -d Ubuntu -- bash -c "cd ~/worm-night/wfit/tools && ~/worm-night/wfit/venv/bin/python fit1_status.py ~/worm-night/wfit/fit1"'`
  (`tools/` holds this branch's fit1_status.py and fit1_loss.py, numpy only. The copy in repo_v1 also works but
  lacks the zero baseline.)
- It prints, for `main` (real wiring) and `shuf` (the wrong model):
  - generations done and the time of the last one;
  - evaluations per hour (measured from history.csv);
  - 10-generation means of the median and best training loss, and of the zero predictor's loss on the same
    batches (zero_loss_10gen_means);
  - sigma;
  - the current mean as values, and any value within 5% of a range end;
  - generations left before the deadline;
  - DONE with its reason, if stopped.
- Read the loss as 10-generation means only, against zero_loss_10gen_means. Each generation scores a different
  mini-batch of 4 stimulations, and the batches differ in difficulty: the zero predictor scores 3.45 on
  generation 0's batch and 7.36 on generation 1's (measured; 4.72 on all training data). The template is about
  the zero predictor (generation 0 median 3.452 against zero 3.454), so the fit's progress is the gap below zero.
- The same split into off-diagonal terms (other neurons' responses, where the wiring matters) and autoresponses
  (about 4 terms per batch, each badly missed). The wiring question is the off-diagonal gap, main's against shuf's.
  Generations 0-2 (measured 02:41): off-diagonal median 4.1902 main, 4.1969 shuf, zero 4.1995; autoresponse 131.3
  main, 132.2 shuf, zero 135.9. About 79% of main's gain over zero so far is in the 4 autoresponse terms (derived).
- The fitted answer is `mean.json` (`mean_vals`), not the best single candidate.
- Backhouse helpers (in `scripts/worm/fit1/`): `bh_sync.sh` copies the code, `bh_launch.sh` starts both fits, `bh_stop.sh` stops them by recorded PID.
- Raw files: `history.csv`, `gen_NNNN/result.json` (per-candidate scores), `driver.log`, `DONE`.
- To stop: `touch ~/worm-night/wfit/fit1/main/STOP` (and `shuf/STOP`). The fit stops after the current
  generation; to resume, delete STOP and rerun the launch line. Stop only by STOP or by the PIDs in
  `drivers.txt` and `*/pids.txt`, never by pattern.
- If backhouse rebooted (drivers gone, no DONE): rerun step 5 of the procedure above. Each fit resumes from state.pkl and keeps the finished evaluations of the generation in progress. A fit that had already stopped stops again at once, because the same rule fires on the same history.
- Held-out scoring is a separate, one-time step after both fits are DONE (`fit1_eval.py`, FIT1.md section 7). Run it from `~/worm-night/wfit/repo_v1`, the fits' own tree; main has since changed values in the model.

## Open

- Neuron Ca did not move on the old code (cainternm.mod fed the current density, 99x too little for AVAL). Fixed
  on main by B1 (af19710, mech/capool.py, in merge 1); the fit now reads the brain's own Ca (FIT1 Amendment 2).
- Quantal noise (row 26, on by default since merge 1) cannot be shared between a stimulated run and its control
  twin. The light changes the voltage-graded vesicle rates, so the Poisson draws shift for the rest of the run.
  Spontaneously active cells then differ by up to 1.31 dF/F between the twins (IL2DL; SDQR 0.23), against
  s_floor 0.03 (measured, template, 10 s windows). With quantal noise off, the responses are the wired ones. The fit
  runs with both noise forms off (they keep the mean, and both state a validity limit of about 1 ms steps).
- Levamisole (Randi's immobilisation) is an L-AChR agonist; the fit does not model it (no hook for a bath agonist
  in wsyn).
- Early drivers of the loss (measured, rank correlation of loss with value within each generation, 3 generations of 16, so noisy): in both fits a larger actuator conductance lowers the loss (obs_opto_g_nS, r -0.63 main, -0.60 shuf), and more gap-junction channels raise it (+0.42, +0.44); in main a larger free Ca fraction lowers it (capool_f, -0.45). So the fit is reaching for the autoresponses through the readout first, which the wiring does not decide. syn_exc_Vth's mean is within 5% of its top (-20.5 of -20 mV) in main.
- The template's responses to stimulation are short and small. Autoresponse 30 s mean 0.014-0.018 (ASHL, AWAL;
  actuator 1 nS) against a training median of 0.43. Peak dF/F 0.24-0.31, gone within about 3 s (measured,
  dtcheck c). It reaches the data only near the upper bounds of the readout values: capool_f 0.003, capool_tca
  150 and the actuator/GCaMP upper bounds give 0.64 and 1.09 (measured, reach2). So capool_f and capool_tca were
  added to the vector before any scored run (FIT1.md, Amendment). AVAL stays at 0.05 even then.
- No per-innexin gap conductance exists in the code (one global open-channel count x one unitary conductance),
  so the brief's "per innexin class" is one value.
- The actuator's ranges (obs_opto_*) and GCaMP6s's half-decay range are guessed, and Randi imaged nuclear
  GCaMP6s, which decays more slowly than the cytosolic range assumes (request below).
