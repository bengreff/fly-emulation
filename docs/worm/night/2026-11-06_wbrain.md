## Registry (committed 20:27)

`src/wormemu/mech/__init__.py`: `REGISTRY`, `Ctx`, `all_specs()`, `all_mechanisms()`, `merge`, `params_for`,
`G_SCALE_NAMES`, `OVERRIDES`. The full contract is the module docstring. Points the other workers need:

- A module's `Mech` is built only when one of its MECHANISMS toggles is not "off". Toggle values may be named forms
  ("well_mixed", ...); anything but "off" is on. Registry toggles the ledger has no row for yet get the module's
  default, so you can code before C rebuilds the ledger.
- `params` = the ledger's value where the ledger has the row, else your Spec's template.
- `g_scale` multipliers are per step (a conductance returns to 1 when no mechanism returns it this step) and multiply
  across mechanisms. Names now: `dens_<channel>` for the 16 BAAIWorm channels, `leak`. More come with row 7
  (per receptor class, presynaptic release).
- `out` keys sum across mechanisms (dicts merge by key). A key in `OVERRIDES` (pumps, eggs_laid, expulsions,
  pharynx_activation, enteric_activation, sex_muscle_activation, gcamp_dff, pressure_scale) replaces the lumped
  layer's value in BrainToBody. All outputs are on `brain.mech_out` after each step; `ctx.outputs` shows the previous
  step's outputs updated by earlier modules this step.
- A registered MECHANISMS id equal to a brain_layers id (e.g. `pharynx`, `peptides`) takes that toggle over: the old
  lumped layer stops running.
- The brain now compiles every `.mod` under `src/wormemu/nmodl/` including subfolders (B2's `nmodl/organs/`).
- Test: `tests/test_worm_mech.py` (dummy module: 30 pA reaches AVBL, g_scale doubles its leak, outputs reach
  `brain.mech_out`, toggle off builds nothing; SPECS and MECHANISMS reach `all_specs`/`all_mechanisms`).

For C (build_ledger): read `mech.all_specs()` and `mech.all_mechanisms()` alongside brain_layers'; a registry id that
equals a brain_layers id replaces it.

### Row 26, channel noise (written 22:31, before any code ran)
Model: Linaro, Storace and Giugliano 2011 (PLoS Comput Biol 7:e1001102, opened), the diffusion approximation in its
conductance form: the gating variables stay deterministic and each channel population's conductance gets a sum of
Ornstein-Uhlenbeck terms whose variances and time constants are the exact second-order statistics of N independent
channels at the current voltage. For a channel open when all its independent two-state gates are open (p = prod
x_k^n_k), the open-state autocovariance is prod (x_k^2 + x_k(1 - x_k) e^(-t/tau_k))^n_k - p^2; expanding it gives one
term per nonzero (j_1..j_K), weight prod C(n_k, j_k) x_k^(2(n_k - j_k)) (x_k(1 - x_k))^j_k, rate sum j_k/tau_k (Linaro's
product-of-covariances rule; 7 terms for m^3 h, 4 for n^4, as in the paper). Applied to BAAIWorm's 15 gated channel
mods at each neuron's soma (NCA has no gate in the model: no noise); weighted forms (SHL-1 0.7 hf + 0.3 hs, KQT-3,
EGL-36) are subpopulations. N = density x soma area / unit conductance; the unit conductances are guessed per
family. x_inf and tau are each mod's RANGE values at the current voltage; the terms are integrated exactly over the
1 ms brain step (OU and its integral, Gillespie 1996) and injected as the step-mean current -dG (V - E) at the soma.
Forms: diffusion (default) / off. Muscles are excluded (B2's).
  1. Code check: the term weights sum to p - p^2 within 1e-12 for every channel form at random gate values.
  2. Against an exact Markov population (N = 200 channels of independent two-state subunits, binomial transitions at

     fraction variance within 3% of the OU sum's, and the normalised autocorrelation at 1, 5 and 20 ms within 0.03.
  3. Step mean: the 1 ms step-mean of one OU term (tau 0.5, 5 and 500 ms) has variance within 3% of the analytic
