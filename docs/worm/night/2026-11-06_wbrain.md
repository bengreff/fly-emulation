## Registry (committed 20:27)

`src/wormemu/mech/__init__.py`: `REGISTRY`, `Ctx`, `all_specs()`, `all_mechanisms()`, `merge`, `params_for`,
`G_SCALE_NAMES`, `OVERRIDES`. The full contract is the module docstring. Points the other workers need:

- A module's `Mech` is built only when one of its MECHANISMS toggles is not "off". Toggle values may be named forms
  ("well_mixed", ...); anything but "off" is on. Registry toggles the ledger has no row for yet get the module's
  default, so you can code before C rebuilds the ledger.
- `params` = the ledger's value where the ledger has the row, else your Spec's template.
- `g_scale` multipliers are per step (a conductance returns to 1 when no mechanism returns it this step) and multiply
  across mechanisms. Names now: `dens_<channel>` for the 16 BAAIWorm channels, `leak`. More come with row 7
  (per receptor class, presynaptic release).
- `out` keys sum across mechanisms (dicts merge by key). A key in `OVERRIDES` (pumps, eggs_laid, expulsions,
  pharynx_activation, enteric_activation, sex_muscle_activation, gcamp_dff, pressure_scale) replaces the lumped
  layer's value in BrainToBody. All outputs are on `brain.mech_out` after each step; `ctx.outputs` shows the previous
  step's outputs updated by earlier modules this step.
- A registered MECHANISMS id equal to a brain_layers id (e.g. `pharynx`, `peptides`) takes that toggle over: the old
  lumped layer stops running.
- The brain now compiles every `.mod` under `src/wormemu/nmodl/` including subfolders (B2's `nmodl/organs/`).
- Test: `tests/test_worm_mech.py` (dummy module: 30 pA reaches AVBL, g_scale doubles its leak, outputs reach
  `brain.mech_out`, toggle off builds nothing; SPECS and MECHANISMS reach `all_specs`/`all_mechanisms`).

For C (build_ledger): read `mech.all_specs()` and `mech.all_mechanisms()` alongside brain_layers'; a registry id that
equals a brain_layers id replaces it.

## Unit-check bars (written before running)

- Row 7 (20:43): UNC-49 class (B/C), saturating GABA step (cleft 10 mM, 1 s, post clamped at -60 mV): the current's
  decay to steady state fits one exponential with tau within 10% of 149 ms (Bamber et al. 2005, HEK, slow component
  149 +- 10 ms). A code check: the tau is an input. Same step on L-AChR and ACR-16: decay-tau ratio between 20 and
  80 ("about 40x", PMC10480131), also by construction. Composite check, not by construction: a 2 ms presynaptic step
  to +50 mV at an NMJ GABA synapse at the template values gives an IPSC whose decay tau is within 24.8 +- 2.7 ms
  (Liu et al. 2009 Fig. 2B); release state, cleft concentration, Hill saturation and tau_off all shape it.
- Rows 9, 10, 28 (21:06), all on the module alone (scripts/worm/neuromod_unit_check.py), template values:
  1. Code check: a receptor at c = its EC50 has occupancy 0.5 (within 1e-9); MOD-5 and DAT-1 uptake at c = Km runs at
     half Vmax (within 1e-9).
  2. Sanity check, conservation and exchange: 1e6 FLP-11 molecules put in the head compartment, no release,
     degradation and coelomocyte uptake off: total molecules conserved to 1e-9 relative over 20000 s; the head's
     excess over the mean decays with the slowest exchange time derived from the geometry (the slowest nonzero
     eigenvalue of the exchange matrix) within 5%; at the end every compartment is within 1% of the mean.
  3. Limit test: the compartment form with diffusion 1e11 um2/s equals the well_mixed form (concentrations and G
     states within 1e-6 relative) over 5 s of the same voltage drive. (Changed from 1e8 before any run: at 1e8 the
     compartments mix with a time constant near 1 ms, the step, so the forms differ by one step's release.)
  4. Sanity check, RIS self-limiting (PORT_SOURCES N10 (g)): RIS held at 0 mV, every other cell at -80 mV, 600 s.
     Bar: head FLP-11 reaches a plateau (change over the last 60 s under 1%); with the RIS DMSR-1 autoreceptor the
     plateau is lower than with it removed, and RIS Gi is above 0.1 with it and 0 without. This shows the negative
     feedback exists, not that a quiescence bout has the right length (that needs RIS's own voltage, so the loop).
  5. Neutral equivalence (tests/test_worm_neuromod.py, NEURON): the lumped form equals brain_layers' old lumped
     layer, voltages within 1e-9 mV over 300 ms.
