## Registry (committed 20:27)

`src/wormemu/mech/__init__.py`: `REGISTRY`, `Ctx`, `all_specs()`, `all_mechanisms()`, `merge`, `params_for`,
`G_SCALE_NAMES`, `OVERRIDES`. The full contract is the module docstring. Points the other workers need:

- A module's `Mech` is built only when one of its MECHANISMS toggles is not "off". Toggle values may be named forms
  ("well_mixed", ...); anything but "off" is on. Registry toggles the ledger has no row for yet get the module's
  default, so you can code before C rebuilds the ledger.
- `params` = the ledger's value where the ledger has the row, else your Spec's template.
- `g_scale` multipliers are per step (a conductance returns to 1 when no mechanism returns it this step) and multiply
  across mechanisms. Names now: `dens_<channel>` for the 16 BAAIWorm channels, `leak`. More come with row 7
  (per receptor class, presynaptic release).
- `out` keys sum across mechanisms (dicts merge by key). A key in `OVERRIDES` (pumps, eggs_laid, expulsions,
  pharynx_activation, enteric_activation, sex_muscle_activation, gcamp_dff, pressure_scale) replaces the lumped
  layer's value in BrainToBody. All outputs are on `brain.mech_out` after each step; `ctx.outputs` shows the previous
  step's outputs updated by earlier modules this step.
- A registered MECHANISMS id equal to a brain_layers id (e.g. `pharynx`, `peptides`) takes that toggle over: the old
  lumped layer stops running.
- The brain now compiles every `.mod` under `src/wormemu/nmodl/` including subfolders (B2's `nmodl/organs/`).
- Test: `tests/test_worm_mech.py` (dummy module: 30 pA reaches AVBL, g_scale doubles its leak, outputs reach
  `brain.mech_out`, toggle off builds nothing; SPECS and MECHANISMS reach `all_specs`/`all_mechanisms`).

For C (build_ledger): read `mech.all_specs()` and `mech.all_mechanisms()` alongside brain_layers'; a registry id that
equals a brain_layers id replaces it.

### Row 26, quantal release noise (written 22:08, before running)
Model: Liu, Hollopeter and Jorgensen 2009 (PNAS 106:10823, PMC2705609, opened): at rest the NMJ releases ACh quanta at
26.3 +- 5.4 /s, 17.6 +- 0.9 pA at -60 mV, and the rate falls (10.2 /s) and rises (35.3 /s) with motor-neuron voltage:
"tonically release ... up- or down-regulated by membrane potential". So the minis ARE the graded release, and the
port makes each wsyn's graded conductance the shot noise of its vesicles with the same mean (not a fixed-rate
generator added on top, which would double the mean). Per synapse: vesicle rate lambda = g_det / (g_q tau), g_q =
17.6 pA / (E_lacr + 60 mV) = 0.248 nS (derived) times the class's single-channel ratio (inferred), tau = the class's
tau_off (graded mode: Liu's evoked decay 5.6 ms); each vesicle adds g_q a (a gamma, mean 1, CV guessed 0.3) decaying
with tau; the synapse's conductance is g_det times qn = G_shot / G_mean (exact mean 1 given the rate history).
Forms: quantal (default) / off (qn = 1, the deterministic synapse). Seeded from the worm's seed.
  1. Generator (PORT_SOURCES N9's isolated check): one synapse whose rate is 26.3 /s, 1000 s: vesicles per s within
     2% of 26.3; mean event peak current at -60 mV within 2% of 17.6 pA (lacr class).
  2. Mean and variance: at rates 26.3, 1e3 and 1e5 /s for 1000 s, mean qn within 1% of 1; variance of G within 3%
     of the exact discrete-time formula and within 10% of Campbell's continuous one, lambda g_q^2 (1 + CV^2) tau / 2.
  3. Limits: g_q scaled down 100x gives the qn standard deviation 10x smaller (within 5%); off gives qn exactly 1.
  4. Seeds: the same seed replays exactly; another seed differs.
  5. Brain (tests): off equals the brain without the module within 1e-12 mV over 300 ms (qn = 1 is neutral in the
     mod); quantal on runs finite. NEURON check: one wsyn, postsynaptic clamp at -60 mV, presynapse held at rest, 20 s:
     mean current with noise within 2% of the deterministic current.
  Reported, not a bar (synapse strengths are one guessed template): the model's implied resting vesicle rate onto a
  body-wall muscle, against Liu's 26.3 /s.
