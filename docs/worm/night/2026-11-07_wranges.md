1. **glia K+ buffer runs away** (src/wormemu/mech/glia.py, `glia_kbuffer = kir`): it injects g_K x dE_K as a fixed
   current, not a conductance, so a cell can be driven below any E_K (-384 mV in seed 2). Ending K+ is floored at
   0.001 x bath (5 uM), below the hard K+ window of 1-30 mM. The "K+ leak" g_K (template 0.2 nS) is not part of any
   membrane: the amphid neurons' whole passive leak is 0.07-0.09 nS (R_pas 11-14 GOhm, BAAIWorm fits). Suggest:
   apply the shift as a conductance on the K+ part of the cell's own leak, and floor Ko at 1 mM.

5. ph_sh_temp_K should come from the world temperature.
6. ph_sh_exp2_hinf_dv has a 1 mV slope, below the 1.5 mV floor of any measured gate.
7. BAAIWorm mods write uM/um2 or uM/um3 for Ca constants that their formulas use as uM; they are read as uM here.
8. The c302 K reversals above -23 mV are impossible (clipped in step 1).
