## Open

- Liu 2018's Kir current as printed (I_Kir = -g_Kir(beta log[1+exp(-(v-v_K+I0)/beta)] + I0)) is inward at every
  voltage under the usual sign convention (C dv/dt = -sum I); ported as printed. A smooth-minimum reading
  (-g (I0 - softplus)) would make it outward at depolarised v; untested, and a candidate for the AWA amplitude fail.
- AWA (Liu 2018) is not all-or-none in my port: at twice the threshold step the first spike is 38% bigger, then
  the cell sits in a plateau near -12 mV (depolarisation block) for ~450 ms.
- The cyclase-route cells (ASEL, AFD, URX, BAG, light) change Po by at most ~10% for the stimuli used (gain A 3,
  guessed); Usuyama's cascade saturates its CNG output near rest. Real AFD/URX responses are large.
- AWB is an OFF cell for nonanone here (Ha et al. 2010 hyperpolarising response), not ON as in brain_layers.
- URX tonic O2 signalling: the cascade adapts, so a sustained 21% O2 does not give URX's measured tonic activity.
- Sanzeni eq 47 printed R_so = r_so exp(d a beta dG); detailed balance with eq 10 would need (1+d) a. Ported as
  printed.
- Sanzeni's g_h and g_s act on a deformation normalised to the bead's own field: not mappable onto a midline body,
  so touch g0 and g1 are guessed.
- Gentle touch is weak: a 10 uN touch on two midline samples gives 0.042 nS (about 4 pA at -54 mV), because only
  the channels at those samples feel it (4 of 50). Measured TRN MRCs are tens of pA (O'Hagan 2005, value not
  opened); the channel count (50, guessed) or the indentation spread along the body is probably too low.
- The filament (1.4 ms) is faster than the 4 ms exchange: touch responses depend on how fast the body delivers the
  force, and a force step reaches the channel as a 4 ms ramp.

## Unit-check bars (written before running)

- **AWA (Liu 2018), 20:38.** Isolated published WT cell (C 1.5 pF), 1 s current steps of 1 to 20 pA from rest: the
  smallest step that fires gives a spike with threshold -34 +/- 5 mV (threshold = v where dv/dt first exceeds
  1 mV/ms), amplitude (peak minus threshold) 25.8 +/- 6 mV and half-width (at threshold + amplitude/2) 18 +/- 6 ms;
  and it is all-or-none (the first spike's amplitude at twice that step within 20% of it). Measured: Liu 2018
  threshold -34.0 +/- 0.6 mV, amplitude 25.8 +/- 0.8 mV, half-width 18.0 +/- 0.8 ms; the wider bands allow for my
  feature definitions differing from theirs. Run on the CPU reference (scipy) and on a NEURON one-section cell with
  the mod; the two must agree within 1 mV on the first spike's peak.
- **AWC (Usuyama 2012), 20:43.** The published protocol (odorant 1000 during 10-70, 280-460 and 670-970 s, else
  0.001) from the published initial state: (a) Ca falls during each pulse; (b) Ca peaks within 10 s after each
  odour removal and above the pre-pulse level; (c) the peak after the 300 s pulse exceeds the peak after the 60 s
  pulse (the paper's claim: peak grows with stimulus duration); (d) my semi-implicit integrator (h 0.25 ms) matches
  scipy LSODA on the same equations within 2% of each peak. (a)-(c) are the paper's reported behaviour of its own
  model (published model output, the measurement it was fitted to is Suzuki et al. 2008-type AWC imaging).
