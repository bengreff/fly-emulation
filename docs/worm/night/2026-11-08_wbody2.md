  Verdict for item 2 (inferred from these runs): two stops in series. (1) MC never fires (brain-owned: MC's membrane
  and drive; Requests). (2) Even a firing MC does not make a pump unless it stays depolarised about 200 ms:
  MC's synapses land on the passive marginal cells (Cook 2019), whose 2-contact gaps (5 nS per contact, guessed) feed
  a syncytium of 20 coupled muscles. Stage (2) is mine; classification pending the EAT-2 localisation source.

- 20:31-20:53 item 2, stage (2). Literature (one Sonnet agent, `docs/worm/night/wbody2/lit_pump_dmp_bwm.md`; I
  re-opened McKay 2004 and Gao & Zhen 2011 and the quotes match): EAT-2, the nicotinic receptor MC needs, "is
  localized to the synapse between pharyngeal muscle and the main pharyngeal excitatory motor neuron MC" (McKay et al.
  2004, PMC1470703, abstract); MC "synapses solely onto marginal cells" (pharynx connectome, bioRxiv 868513). Off food:
  "to about one-quarter of the rate on food (i.e., 50 pumps per minute)" in the first 120 min of food deprivation,
  and on food "more than 200 pumps per minute" (Dallière et al. 2016 FASEB J, PMC4714553, their own measurements;
  re-opened). So the off-food bar is about 0.8 Hz (derived from 50 per minute), the on-food bar 3.46 +- 0.19 Hz.
  Wiring (measured from the built model): MC's 41 contacts all land on the three mc2 marginal cells (MCL 20, MCR 21),
  and mc2's gaps reach pm5 and mc1/mc3, not pm4; at 0.447 nS per contact that is at most 18 nS from MC (derived).
  Probe `scripts/worm/wbody2/pharynx_coupling_probe.py` (bar in its docstring, written before running), 40 pA x 30 ms
  into MCL and MCR at 4 Hz, 3 bio s, 9 s wall per point: results in the status table
  (`runs/worm/wbody2/pharynx_coupling_probe*.json`, not committed). Why the 2 nS runs stop after 2 APs (measured,
  gap 5 nS, area 500): pm4 rests at -69 to -70 mV, 3-4 mV above the leak reversal (-73 mV, Sherman & Harel's measured
  interpump potential), held up by the pharyngeal neurons (gaps and tonic graded synapses; they rest at -56 to -68 mV).
  CCA-1's inactivation gate h_inf = 1/(1+exp((V+76)/4)) is 0.32 at -73 mV and 0.18 at -70 mV (derived); it read 0.19
  at the first pulse (still relaxing from the -73 mV start), fell to 0.02 after the two APs and was back to only 0.08
  at 2.75 s, because each subthreshold MC event (pm4 to -45 mV) re-inactivates it (tau 250 ms). The first AP is
  partly a start-up effect.
  Verdict for stage (2): B. The model's MC -> muscle step is BAAIWorm's generic graded synapse at the body-wall NMJ
  value per contact onto passive marginal cells; the published step is the EAT-2/EAT-18 nicotinic synapse at the
  MC-muscle junction (McKay 2004), which our model has no form of, and Sherman & Harel's muscle was fitted to one TB
  AP, not to trains at 4 Hz from an in-situ rest (validity range). No in-range value of the three couplings gives one
  pump per MC event, so it is not D. Nothing adopted; nothing changed in the model for stage (2). What a port would
  need: an EAT-2 receptor form (no free kinetic model found yet) and a check that the S&H muscle fires trains at
  3-4 Hz. Stage (1), MC silent, comes first and is wbrain2's (Requests).
