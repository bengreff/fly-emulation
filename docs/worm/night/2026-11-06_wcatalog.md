## New finding: the BAAIWorm channel files hard-code their reversal potentials

Reading the mod files for the hidden scan: 11 of the 12 potassium channels compute their current against a fixed
-80 mV, not against the ion's reversal; unc2, egl19, cca1, the SLO nanodomain formulas and cainternm use a fixed
+60 mV for calcium; nca declares that it reads the sodium reversal but computes against +30 mV. Only kqt3 reads the
potassium reversal (ion_ek, -77 mV here), so kqt3 and the other K channels sit 3 mV apart. Consequences: ion_ena,
ion_cao and the temperature row reach no current (no Q10 is built yet, so the channels are temperature-independent),
and the brain.py docstring's Ca units fix changes nothing that runs. These literals are now 11 hidden rows,
published_fit as ported: baai_ek_mV, baai_eca_mV, baai_ena_nca_mV, and the shl1, kqt3 and egl36 fraction and time
scales (egl36's three weights sum to 1.08, not 1). The ion rows carry owner notes for B1.
