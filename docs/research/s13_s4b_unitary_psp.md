# Measured unitary PSPs at insect central and premotor→MN synapses

"Verified" = the subagent read the number in the source text. "Derived" = the subagent's own division of a PSP by
an EM synapse count. No adult Drosophila premotor→leg-MN unitary PSP was found (gap, as the S4b pre-registration
assumed).

**Not found or not verified:** Gouwens & Wilson 2009 (PN uEPSP and Rin); Liu & Wilson 2013; giant fiber→TTMn/PSI;
Zwart 2016 GDL→MN; Agrawal 2020; Laurent & Burrows 1989 amplitudes; cockroach and stick-insect (Büschges) values.

**Summary (subagent):** well-documented insect central connections give unitary PSPs of about 0.4-10 mV per
presynaptic spike, most in 1-7 mV; locust FETi→flexor (12-24 mV) is a secure MN→MN outlier. mV per synapse, where
derivable, is about 0.1-0.3 in small Drosophila central neurons, and is not a constant (location and density matter).
Those postsynaptic cells are much smaller than leg MNs (KC >10 GΩ; leg MN 150-700 MΩ, Azevedo 2020), so per-synapse
depolarisation at a leg MN is expected to be at or below that band, not above it (inference, not measured).
