# s23 reads: an independent N28 temperature check, and N26 re-entrainment after an LD shift (24 October 2026)

One Sonnet reader (reader 3), primary full text through PMC and Europe PMC unless marked. No survival times, intake
totals, rebound magnitudes or MBON/DAN rates were reported. Used in MECHANISM_STATUS (N28, N26 paths), 24 Oct.

## N28: temperature dependence of a neural latency or rate, other than Juusola & Hardie 2001
- **Giant-fibre pathway, Iyengar, Ruan & Wu 2022** eNeuro 9:ENEURO.0443-21.2021 (PMC8805199), Table 1: GF→DLM
  short-latency response 1.1 ± 0.3 ms (n 9, reared 25 °C) and 1.3 ± 0.3 ms (n 13, reared 29 °C); long-latency
  4.1 ± 0.4 and 4.3 ± 0.4 ms. Canton-S, about 7 d. **Recording always at 21-23 °C**, so this is rearing
  temperature, not an acute Q10. Not an N28 check.
- **AC hot-sensing neuron, Alpert, Gil, Para & Gallio 2022** Curr Biol 32:4079 (PMC9529852), Fig. 1C-E: whole-cell,
  "little AC firing at or below 25°C (<1Hz)", threshold about 27.2 °C, 9 cells / 6 animals, acute ramps 20-33 °C.
  This is thermosensory transduction, not the passive scaling of membrane and synaptic kinetics N28 models. Not an
  N28 check.
- ERG and photoreceptor temperature series other than Juusola: Kelly & Suzuki 1974 (PMC434008) and Siddiqi &
  Benzer 1976 (PMC430996) are scanned pages without text; the PDF route is behind a proof-of-work check, not used.
- Larval NMJ kinetics against temperature: Feng, Ueda & Wu 2004 (block temperature only) and Newman, Xiao &
  Robertson 2005 (cross-species), abstracts only. None found.
- **Result: no independent N28 check in open full text.** N28 keeps its cited range (2.3-3.5) and no check.

## N26: days to re-entrain after an LD phase shift, wild type
- **Koh, Zheng & Sehgal 2006** Science 312:1809 (PMC2767177), Fig. 1B: y w controls (n 13), 12:12 LD, an 8 h
  delay on day 3, actogram with evening onsets marked. The text gives no day count ("they took longer to be
  re-entrained to a new schedule than wild-type flies"). The reader's visual estimate, **not a stated value**:
  wild-type evening onsets move to the new phase over about 3-4 cycles. Sex, age and temperature not stated.
- Not usable for a day count: Lamba 2014 and 2018 (single-pulse PRCs), Levine 2002 (a DD pulse example),
  Kistenpfennig 2012 (PRC), Abhilash 2020 and Miyasako 2007 (temperature-cycle shifts), Sinam 2012 (another species).
- **Status:** Koh 2006 Fig. 1B is now **seen**, so it can only be training for μ, not held out. The model's
  1-day re-entrainment of a 6 h jump (s23 LD check, reported) is faster than this estimate, but the shift size
  (8 h delay against a 6 h start-state jump) and the read (by eye, no stated number) differ. A proper figure read
  of the onset times would be needed before μ is set from it.
