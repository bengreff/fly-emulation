What drives the legs

Nothing is scripted. Motor neurons in the connectome fire, the muscles they are mapped to pull, and MuJoCo resolves the rest. There is no gait, no controller and no descending command.

The parameters are guesses

Every neuron shares one membrane time constant, one threshold and one synaptic strength. The model is short of 19 distinct inferences, propagated to 27.5 million model elements.

How the state was filled

All 176,422 neurons start exactly at rest with no synaptic history; the body starts at its neutral pose 2 mm up. The opening fall and the first activity are transients of that choice, not behaviour.

Two disclosures

Torque per spike was set to 10 µN·mm to make the motion visible, and the command clips against the body’s ±30 limit. Over half the motor output — wing, abdominal, neck, haltere — has no actuator to drive.
